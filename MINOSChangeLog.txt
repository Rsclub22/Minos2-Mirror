2.8.0.0 to 2..8.0.1000 Beta

Apart from bug fixes, the main change is a re-working of the KST app.

It is now broken into multiple panes in the same way as the logger, and 
has its own layout editor. Panes that aren't shown are accessible as 
dialogs under the right mouse button. Like logger, it can have multiple 
"screens".

The layout designer has the ability to set font sizes (as a proportion of the 
overall font size) independently for each panel in the logger and the KST app.
